# Contributor Licence Agreement

Thank you for contributing to Dwara. This agreement lets the project accept your
work and keep it usable, including under a second licence.

**Please have a lawyer read this before you rely on it.** It was drafted from
common practice, not by counsel.

## Why this exists

Dwara is licensed under the AGPL-3.0-or-later. That is the right default: it keeps
the project open, and it means anyone who builds Dwara into a product they
distribute or offer over a network has to open their own work in turn.

Some organisations cannot accept the AGPL. To offer them a separate commercial
licence, the project needs the right to license contributed code under terms other
than the AGPL. Without this agreement, every past contributor would have to be
found and asked individually, which in practice means it never happens.

You keep the copyright in your contribution. This is a licence, not an assignment.

## How to agree

Add this line to the description of your first pull request:

```
I have read CLA.md and I agree to it.
```

One agreement covers all your future contributions. If the terms change, you will
be asked again before anything you have already submitted is used under new terms.
